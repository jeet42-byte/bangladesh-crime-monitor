# Study 1: observations made while building the frame

These are **case observations**, not measured outcomes. They come from frame-building (script plus browser checks, Nov 2026). They may be reported descriptively in the paper. **No institution is named** in the paper for a weakness (protocol §9).

## Lapsed, moved or wrongly listed domains (`frame/legacy_domains.csv`)
- One bank's former domain is **listed for sale**.
- One renamed university's former domain **no longer resolves** (it is available for re-registration).
- **The UGC private-university list gives non-existent or outdated domains for at least 4 universities.**
- Several institutions moved domains, e.g. `.org.bd` → `.gov.bd`, or `.edu.bd` → `.ac.bd`.

## Official-looking but non-Bangladeshi domains
- Universities using `.ac`: this is **Ascension Island's** country code, not an academic suffix.
- A university using `ac-bd.org`, a `.org` name that imitates `.ac.bd`.
- Generic TLDs: `.education`, `.university`.

## Free webmail in official contact details
- Free webmail (Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail) appears in official contact details for:
  - 1 of 75 financial institutions
  - 2 of 88 government bodies
  - **21 of 162 universities**

## Website state
- 14 of 88 government sites fail standard certificate verification. Several financial sites do too, and some are HTTP-only.
- Sites unreachable at frame time: 2 government, 1 university.
- One university serves an empty directory listing.

## Template leftovers / unexplained third-party domains
- 2 university sites contain template placeholder domains.
- 2 university sites reference unrelated third-party domains (possible injected links). **To verify by hand before any mention.**
