WAR DOGS · ACTING PROMPTS FOR IMAGE-TO-VIDEO (silent clips)

How to use
1. Open a free image-to-video tool on your laptop (Kling AI, Hailuo / MiniMax, Luma Dream Machine or Pika).
2. Upload the image named below (from organized-crime-assignment/3d_scene_images/ or your own copies).
3. Paste the prompt. Settings: 16:9, 5–10 seconds, sound OFF, no text.
4. Download the MP4 and name it like the image (title.mp4, s01.mp4 ... s12.mp4). Send them back here.
Add this to the end of every prompt if the tool allows: "Keep the characters' faces, clothes and the setting exactly as in the image. Pixar-style 3D animation. No speech bubbles, no text, no new characters."
